\section{Executive Summary}
% =========================================================================

Frontier and emerging capital markets have historically suffered from severe structural friction: acute information asymmetry, fragmented discourse channels, low retail engagement, and sluggish dissemination of market-moving corporate disclosures. While Western markets have matured platforms such as Bloomberg Terminal, Stocktwits, and Twitter/X Finance, emerging markets such as the Nigerian Exchange (NGX) have lacked a unified, high-integrity platform that harmonizes real-time tape prints, corporate filings, verified analyst commentary, and retail consensus.

\textbf{Liquidity Square} directly resolves these structural deficiencies. The application creates a cohesive capital markets terminal and social intelligence environment that pairs high-velocity financial microblogging with verifiable market data.

\begin{tcolorbox}[title=Core Mission and Paradigm]
\textbf{The Convergence of Floor Intelligence and Exchange Tape:}
Liquidity Square merges the spontaneous, crowdsourced intelligence of an open trading pit (``The Floor'') with deterministic, low-latency market data streamed from exchange feeds and regulatory document libraries (``The Tape''). By anchoring social discourse to formal corporate tickers (e.g., \texttt{\$DANGCEM}, \texttt{\$GTCO}, \texttt{\$MTNN}), the platform transforms casual market conversation into quantitative sentiment indices, contrarian alpha signals, and compliance-grade regulatory dissemination channels.
\end{tcolorbox}

Key technical and operational pillars identified in the codebase include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Direct Regulatory Ingestion:} Automated OData/XML streaming from the official NGX document repository (\texttt{doclib.ngxgroup.com}), delivering parsed corporate earnings, dividends, AGM notices, and scrip issues within minutes of submission.
    \item \textbf{Real-Time Tape Synchronizer:} Automated NGX equities session tracking operating on West Africa Time (WAT / UTC+1), calculating session-by-session price changes, volume, turnover, high/low prints, and sector aggregate performance.
    \item \textbf{High-Conviction Sentiment Architecture:} Author-level stance tagging (Bull/Bear), binary stance polling, and multi-horizon sentiment windows (Session, 7-Day, 30-Day) deduplicated at the user and ticker levels.
    \item \textbf{Floor vs. Tape Dislocation Analytics:} Proprietary detection of market divergences where price action on the tape contradicts social sentiment on the floor, surfacing high-alpha contrarian setups and uncrowded corporate moves.
    \item \textbf{Professional Credentialing and 60-Day Title Governance:} A catalog of over 627 professional finance titles across 9 capital market disciplines, locked for 60 calendar days upon selection to enforce credibility and deter malicious sockpuppeting.
    \item \textbf{Institutional Pro Rooms:} Curated analyst and institutional desks providing audio/text replays, research notes, and live sector deep dives.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{External Data Ingestion \& Telemetry Pipelines}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Official NGX Equities Tape (\texttt{tape.ts}):}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Consumes raw trading statistics directly from \texttt{https://doclib.ngxgroup.com/REST/api/statistics/equities/}.
        \item Processes daily open, high, low, close, previous close, volume, traded value (turnover), trade count, and calculated percentage changes across all listed equities.
        \item Governed by an intelligent session clock (\texttt{session.ts}) operating on West Africa Time (WAT / UTC+1). The engine recognizes the 09:30--16:00 trading window, executes 30-minute half-hour refreshes, and caches the 16:00 closing print throughout weekends and overnight until the next market open.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Regulatory Disclosure Pipeline (\texttt{ngx-filings.ts}):}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Ingests real-time financial disclosures from the official NGX SharePoint OData/XML endpoint: \texttt{doclib.ngxgroup.com/\_api/Web/Lists/GetByTitle('XFinancial\_News')/items/}.
        \item Automatically classifies unstructured disclosures into categorical taxonomy: \textit{Results} (audited/unaudited earnings), \textit{Dividends} (cash/scrip announcements), \textit{Meetings} (AGM/EGM notices), and \textit{Corporate Filings}.
        \item Generates deterministic SHA-256 filing signatures (\texttt{filingKey = sha256(symbol | url)}) to guarantee exactly-once delivery across client push notifications.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Fundamental Data Synchronizer (\texttt{sync-company-profiles.cjs}):}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Integrates with specialized capital market data providers (\texttt{api.ngnmarket.com}) to populate corporate dossiers: CEO names, executive leadership, date incorporated, date listed, shares outstanding, industry, SIC codes, and corporate addresses.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Decentralized Logo CDN Repository:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Features automated fallback rendering for corporate logos utilizing optimized WebP/PNG formats hosted on CDN repositories with graceful fallbacks to localized typography.
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\section{Master Capability Index: What the Platform Does}
% =========================================================================

Liquidity Square is engineered as an all-in-one financial ecosystem. The following inventory comprehensively details every feature and operational capability implemented across the platform.

\subsection{Real-Time Market Tape \& Session Telemetry}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{NGX Equities Tape Bar:} A continuous top-level market ticker displaying real-time session quotes, price changes, and volume metrics across all 147 NGX-listed equities.
    \item \textbf{Session State Engine:} Automatically models market states (Pre-open, Live Session, Post-close, Weekend) using the Lagos financial calendar, preventing stale data artifacts.
    \item \textbf{Top Movers Leaderboard:} Automatically computes and ranks the top gainers and losers in real time, filtered by volume and absolute percentage movements.
    \item \textbf{Turnover \& Liquidity Leaderboard:} Ranks equities by traded cash value (\texttt{mostTradedByValue}), illuminating where institutional capital is concentrated during each session.
    \item \textbf{Historical Week Strip:} An interactive horizontal carousel embedded across feeds displaying all corporate filings and dividend notices released across the market over the preceding 7 business days.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Trading Floor: High-Conviction Social Discourse}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Multi-Dimensional Feed Hierarchy:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{For You:} Discovery feed aggregating trending discussions, high-engagement market theses, and active market commentary.
        \item \textit{Following:} Curated chronologically sorted feed of followed investors, analysts, and corporate profiles.
        \item \textit{Watchlist Feed:} High-relevance feed filtering posts specifically referencing tickers in the authenticated user's portfolio.
        \item \textit{Verified Feed:} Institutional feed restricted to accredited analysts, verified institutions, and listed corporate representatives.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Smart Cashtag Autocomplete:} Real-time inline regex parser that detects the \texttt{\$} character and renders an interactive popup displaying the stock symbol, company name, live price, and percentage change.
    \item \textbf{Bullish / Bearish Stance Selection:} Enables creators to explicitly tag their thesis with a high-visibility green ``Bull'' or red ``Bear'' indicator, feeding directly into the ticker's quantitative sentiment index.
    \item \textbf{Structured Sentiment Polling:} Empowers users to launch 2-to-4 option market polls with optional Bull/Bear stances attached to individual choices, gathering community forecasts.
    \item \textbf{Content Scheduling \& Server Drafts:} Supports drafting complex financial theses with automatic local persistence, manual draft management, and time-stamped background automated dispatch.
    \item \textbf{Quote Posting \& Deep Threading:} Full recursive conversation trees supporting nested replies, quote posts with live parent previews, and persistent bookmarking.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Stock Ticker Intelligence Rooms}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Dedicated Symbol Portals:} Bespoke pages for every listed ticker (e.g., \texttt{/square/DANGCEM}) integrating live pricing with social volume.
    \item \textbf{Session Statistics Grid:} Tabulation of trading metrics including Day High, Day Low, Traded Volume, Total Traded Value (Turnover in Naira), and Total Trade Count.
    \item \textbf{Corporate Dossier Card:} Deep fundamental facts including CEO, Executive Officers, Founding Year, Date Listed, Date Incorporated, Employee Headcount, SIC Code, Shares Outstanding, and Official Corporate Website.
    \item \textbf{Sector Traversal:} Seamless navigation from individual tickers to comprehensive Sector Rooms (e.g., Banking, Industrial Goods, Consumer Goods, Oil \& Gas).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Sentiment Analytics \& ``Floor vs. Tape'' Contrarian Engine}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Multi-Window Sentiment Gauges:} Real-time visualization of Bull vs. Bear ratios calculated over three configurable horizons:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{Session Window:} Sentiment expressed strictly during the current trading day.
        \item \textit{7-Day Window:} Rolling weekly sentiment momentum.
        \item \textit{30-Day Window:} Rolling monthly macro thesis.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Deduplicated Sentiment Indexing:} Prevents spam manipulation by ensuring each user receives exactly one vote per ticker (derived from stance posts or poll votes, with author-vote collision suppression).
    \item \textbf{Floor vs. Tape Dislocation Detection:} An algorithmic engine that scans the market for significant divergences:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{Contrarian Divergence:} Identifies stocks that are rallying on the tape while community sentiment is overwhelmingly bearish, or crashing on the tape while sentiment is heavily bullish.
        \item \textit{Uncrowded Movers:} Flags stocks experiencing large price deviations ($\ge 5\%$) with zero social volume, pinpointing overlooked institutional accumulation or distribution.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Regulatory Disclosure Engine \& Push Alerts}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Direct Exchange Scraper:} Automated background pollers querying the NGX SharePoint API at regular intervals.
    \item \textbf{Automated Delivery Ledger:} High-speed insertion into \texttt{DisclosureReceipt} ensuring users never receive duplicate push notifications for the same filing.
    \item \textbf{Direct Linkage to Exchange Documents:} Every disclosure notification contains direct, verified links to official PDF financial statements and corporate filings.
    \item \textbf{Customizable Price Threshold Alerts:} User-configured alerts triggered when a watched equity moves beyond a designated percentage threshold ($0.5\%$ to $50\%$) in a specified direction (\textit{Up}, \textit{Down}, or \textit{Either Way}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Analyst Pro Rooms \& Community Desks}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Pro Rooms (Virtual Trading Desks):} Specialized virtual environments hosted by senior fund managers, macro strategists, and institutional desks (e.g., ``Banking Sector: Recapitalisation Deep Dive'').
    \item \textbf{Desk Notes \& Audio Summaries:} Pinned analytical briefs, generation-volume checks, and valuation models shared during live market sessions.
    \item \textbf{Curated Financial Lists:} Followable, themed lists of verified market participants (e.g., ``NGX Banks Desk'', ``Dividend Hunters'', ``Macro Watch'').
    \item \textbf{Communities:} Segmented forums dedicated to specialized market segments such as ``The Floor'' (open market pit), ``First Trades'' (newcomer education), and ``FX Desk'' (currency impacts on equities).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Professional Identity \& Governance}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{627 Professional Finance Titles:} A rigorous, curated taxonomy of capital market titles spanning Enthusiasts, Equities, Fixed Income, FX \& Commodities, Alternatives \& Private Equity, Investment Banking, Wealth Management, Risk \& Compliance, and C-Suite Executives.
    \item \textbf{60-Day Anti-Fraud Title Lock:} Once a user selects or updates their title, the platform cryptographically locks the field for 60 calendar days (\texttt{TITLE\_LOCK\_DAYS = 60}). This eliminates deceptive credential-flipping and creates professional reputational accountability.
    \item \textbf{Institutional Verification Badges:} Dedicated verification badges distinguishing accredited institutional accounts, corporate investor relations handles, and vetted equity research analysts.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Benefits for Broker-Dealers \& Market Makers}
